# Framework fallback

Use this only when the workspace does not contain `PROJECT_EVALUATION_FRAMEWORK.md`.

## Principles

1. Working code is not a validated business.
2. Interest, traffic, registrations, and free usage are not WTP.
3. Regulation, deadlines, and trends are not independent demand.
4. Include paid, free/open-source, platform-native, manual, and do-nothing alternatives.
5. Separate features from the outcome the buyer purchases.
6. Test payment before building features.
7. Avoid guarantees for legal, financial, security, or professional claims.
8. Attach observable continue, pivot, and stop thresholds to every decision.

## Required evaluation

For every candidate analyze:

- buyer, user, price, artifact/service, and purchased outcome;
- frequency, urgency, current behavior, and cost of inaction;
- five categories of alternatives;
- technical versus commercial readiness;
- one-time versus recurring value and contribution-margin path;
- first 50 qualified buyers and repeatable acquisition channel;
- strongest evidence tier and missing primary evidence;
- `GO`, `CONDITIONAL GO`, `PIVOT`, `HOLD`, or `NO-GO`.

## Default paid test

- 10 days;
- 50 qualified personalized contacts;
- at least 10 problem conversations;
- at least 3 real payments or equivalent paid pilots;
- at least one repeatable acquisition channel before scaling.

Add delivery acceptance, logged fulfillment time, contribution margin, customer outcome, and repeat-payment gates whenever the candidate requires service fulfillment.
