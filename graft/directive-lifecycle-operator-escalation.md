## Summary

The human-directive is the single immutable contract between operator and company: directive_writer is its sole writer, enforcing fail-closed rules (PENDING never clobbered without --allow-pending; body immutable after acceptance, only ## Status changes via compare-and-swap on body hash). operator_request_notify is the sole authority for deciding when human input is genuinely needed, validating only seven escalation-worthy types and requiring deterministic verification (checksum-matched evidence, structured logs, or explicit directives) before resolution. directive-staleness-watch alerts when a PENDING directive ages past a threshold, since its completion clause can only be satisfied by market evidence or an explicit terminal decision.
