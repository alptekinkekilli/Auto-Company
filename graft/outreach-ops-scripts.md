A family of Python CLI tools under scripts/ops/ that audit and gate the outreach pipeline: reply-watch classifies email replies/bounces/silence, send-gate enforces fail-closed refusal policy before any send, state-snapshot tracks directive/OPREQ state with DELTA change detection, tool-usage-audit tallies tool usage from jcode NDJSON, and turn-audit enforces the turn-economy policy.
